FORGE — BRAND KIT
Exact values as built, 30 August 2026.
Every number below is read out of the shipping stylesheets, not from memory.
Source files: src/styles.css, src/forge-system.css, src/forge-packages.css

Forge is a strength and endurance training log. Its one idea: you log the TOP
SET — the single heaviest honest effort of a session — and the app coaches the
progression from there. The look is dark, high-contrast, athletic. Not clinical,
not soft, not "wellness".


================================================================================
1. THE SIGNATURE PALETTE  (package name: "Forge" — the default)
================================================================================

This is the app as it appears out of the box and the palette any logo should be
designed against.

  ACCENT / VOLT       #D7FF45     the brand colour. Acid lime-yellow.
  ACCENT INK          #0E1305     text/icons placed ON the accent
  BACKGROUND          #0A0E0D     app ground, near-black with a green cast
  PANEL (card)        #131A18     surface one step up from the ground
  PANEL 2             #1A2320     surface two steps up
  HAIRLINE / LINE     #222D29     all borders and dividers
  TEXT                #EEF4F1     primary text
  MUTED               #9DABA5     secondary text

Status colours (used for meaning, never decoration):

  GOOD / SUCCESS      #58D68D
  WARNING             #F6C453
  BAD / ERROR         #EF8A7B
  INFO                #6FC3FF

Notes for a designer:
  - The accent is a yellow-green, NOT a pure yellow and NOT a mint. It is the
    single loudest element in the app and is used sparingly: buttons, active
    states, key numbers, and the eyebrow labels.
  - #0E1305 (near-black green) is what sits on top of the accent. Never white
    on #D7FF45 — it fails contrast badly.
  - The ground is not pure black. It carries a slight green cast (#0A0E0D).
  - There is a faint accent glow in the app's background:
    radial-gradient(circle at 72% -10%, rgba(215,255,69,.08), transparent 26%)

3. TYPE SCALE
================================================================================

Headings — Barlow Condensed, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.015em:

  H1        clamp(19px, 4vw, 24px)
  H2        clamp(27px, 5vw, 58px)      line-height 0.96
  H3        clamp(19px, 3.4vw, 23px)

Body — Inter:

  Paragraph         16px, line-height 1.55, colour #9DABA5
  Measure           max 62 characters per line

Eyebrow / kicker label (appears above almost every heading in the app):

  font-size: 10px
  font-weight: 800
  letter-spacing: 0.12em
  text-transform: uppercase
  colour: #D7FF45 (the accent)

Numerals:
  font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums  — wherever numbers are compared in a
  column. Any logo or wordmark containing numbers should line up the same way.

================================================================================
5. APP ICON — CURRENT BACKGROUND VALUES
================================================================================

Whatever mark gets designed, these are the grounds it has to sit on:

  Icon background      vertical gradient  #0C110F  →  #060908
  PWA theme colour     #0B100E
  Splash background    #0B100E

Hard constraints from the App Store:
  - 1024 × 1024 px, square, fully opaque, NO alpha channel. An icon with an
    alpha channel is rejected at upload.
  - No rounded corners baked in. iOS applies its own mask; corners drawn into
    the artwork show up as visible notches.
  - It must survive at 29 × 29 px. That is the real design constraint — most
    detail disappears there.

Sizes needed for iOS: 1024, 180, 167, 152, 144, 120, 114, 100, 87, 80, 76, 60,
58, 40, 29, 20. Android/PWA: 512, 384, 256, 192, plus maskable 512 and 192 with
the mark scaled to ~62% so Android's circular crop does not cut it.

================================================================================
7. BRIEF NOTES FOR WHOEVER DESIGNS THE MARK
================================================================================

What the app is:
  A training log built around the top set. Strength and endurance in one place.
  An AI coach that programs from what you actually lifted and ran, never from
  what you told it you could do. The tone throughout the product is direct and
  evidence-first — it says "you have not run enough for me to project that"
  rather than filling space with encouragement.

Who uses it:
  Adults training seriously. Hybrid athletes — people who lift and run — are
  the sharpest niche and the least well served by existing apps.

What already exists that a designer should have:
  - This file.
  - src/forge-icon.svg and src/forge-mark.svg in the repo (current mark).
  - Screenshots of any app screen on request.

Deliverables to ask for:
  - Master vector (SVG or AI), mark alone, on transparent.
  - Mark locked up with the FORGE wordmark, horizontal and stacked.
  - Single-colour version (one flat colour, no gradient) for small sizes and
    for the CLUB / TRAIL light packages.
  - App icon artwork at 1024 × 1024, opaque, square, no baked corners.
  - Proof that it reads at 29 px before anything else is approved.

Things that will look wrong in this product:
  - Gradients into muted or olive tones. #D7FF45 ramped toward anything
    desaturated goes army-green immediately.
  - White or light text sitting directly on the accent.
  - Baked-in rounded corners on the app icon.
  - Anything that relies on fine detail or thin strokes.
